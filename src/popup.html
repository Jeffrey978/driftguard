Let DriftGuard see the page?

Rules can't tell a tutorial from a rabbit hole. An AI check can. It's off unless you turn it on.

  • Sends a screenshot of the visible tab and the page text.
  • Only during focus sessions, once a page has been open a few seconds.
  • Goes to DriftGuard's server, which asks an AI model whether the page fits your task.
  • Nothing is stored there. Only the yes/no answer stays in this browser.
  • Never on sites you exclude or in incognito. Pages with a password field send text only, never a screenshot.
